What technologies do you use?

We select the stack per project: React (Vite), Next.js, Laravel and SvelteKit for web; React Native for mobile; MQTT and WebSocket for IoT.

How long does a typical project take?

A company profile with CMS takes 2–4 weeks. Custom web or mobile applications typically take 2–4 months.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Every project includes post-launch support — 3 months for Basic, 6 months for Small Business, lifetime for Business.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

Yes — payment gateways, WhatsApp APIs, AI services, and any REST or GraphQL backend.
